2. WHAT WE COLLECT & WHY

We only collect information that helps us deliver, secure, and improve our services. To comply with global privacy laws, including the GDPR and various US state laws, we rely on specific legal bases for processing your data.

Information You Give Us Directly

When you create an account or use our services, we collect the following information:

  • Legal Basis: Processing is necessary for the performance of our contract with you, and in some cases, based on your explicit consent.

Information We Collect Automatically

As you use our app, we automatically collect device information (such as your device model, operating system version, and app version) and usage analytics (such as the screens you visit and features you use). We collect this data to ensure our app works correctly on your device and to understand which features our users find most valuable.

  • Legal Basis: We process this data based on our legitimate interest in maintaining a functional, secure, and user-friendly app.

Information from Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with your Google account, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. This allows us to authenticate your identity and create your account.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): We use Firebase Analytics to gather aggregated information about app usage. This service provides us with insights into user behavior, helping us make informed decisions about future updates and improvements.
  • Legal Basis: We rely on your consent (provided when you sign in and accept permissions) and our legitimate interest in understanding app usage patterns.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We put your information to work in specific, purposeful ways. We do not collect data just to have it; every piece of information serves a distinct function.

  • Core Service Delivery: We use your name, email, and age to administer your account, provide our travel planning services (flight search, hotel booking, train search, trip itineraries), and fulfill our obligations to you.
  • Service Improvement and Personalization: By analyzing device information and usage data, we identify bugs, optimize our user interface, and tailor our services to better meet your travel planning needs. This relies on our legitimate interests.
  • Communication: We use your contact details to respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: We monitor usage analytics and device data to detect unusual activity, prevent unauthorized access, and protect our platform from malicious actors. Protecting our ecosystem is a core legitimate interest.
  • Legal Compliance: We may process your information to comply with applicable laws and regulations, fulfilling our legal obligations.

4. WHEN WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We are highly selective about who we share your information with, and we keep data sharing to the absolute minimum required to run our services.

We never sell your personal data.

When we do share your information, it falls into one of these specific categories:

  • Essential Service Providers: We share data with trusted vendors who help us operate, such as Google (for Firebase Authentication and Analytics) and cloud hosting providers. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We will disclose your information if we are legally required to do so by a court order, subpoena, or other lawful request from public authorities.
  • Business Transitions: If Trip Buddy is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your personal data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With Your Consent: If we need to share your data for any purpose not listed above, we will ask for your explicit permission first.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

We adhere to high standards of data protection, including the EU and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, and comprehensive US State Privacy Laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, and CTDPA). Regardless of where you live, we grant you the following rights over your data:

  • Access Your Data: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We will provide this within 30 days.
  • Correct Inaccuracies: If your name, email, age, or other details change or are incorrect, you have the right to ask us to update them.
  • Delete Your Data: You can request that we erase your personal information. We will honor this request unless we have a compelling legal or security reason to retain it.
  • Port Your Data: You can ask us to provide your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format so you can transfer it to another service.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent to process your data, you can withdraw that consent at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of any processing we did before you withdrew consent.
  • Lodge Complaints: If you believe we have violated your privacy rights, you have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at rajabhishek4444@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

6. DATA SECURITY

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect it. We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect data in transit and restrict access to your personal information to authorized personnel only.

However, we want to be completely honest with you: no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

You also play a vital role in keeping your data safe. We encourage you to keep your Google account secure and to ensure the devices you use to access Trip Buddy are protected.

In the unlikely event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we commit to notifying you and the relevant regulatory authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law (typically within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach).

7. DATA RETENTION

We do not keep your data forever. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • Active Account Data: We keep your profile information (name, email, age) for the duration of your use of the app, plus a reasonable buffer of 6 months after account deletion to handle any lingering support requests.
  • Technical Logs and Analytics: We retain device information and usage analytics for a period of 12 months. This gives us enough historical data to identify long-term trends without hoarding unnecessary technical details.

Once these retention periods expire, we securely delete or anonymize your data so it can no longer be linked back to you.
