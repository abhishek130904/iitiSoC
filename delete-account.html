How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Trip Buddy account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to rajabhishek4444@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Trip Buddy account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete My Trip Buddy Account"
  3. In the email body, confirm that you want your account and all associated data to be permanently deleted.
  4. We will verify your identity and process your request within 15 business days.
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.
📧 Send Deletion Request Email

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data will be permanently removed:

Data Type Action Timeframe
Name & Email Address Permanently Deleted Within 15 days
Age / Date of Birth Permanently Deleted Within 15 days
Profile Information Permanently Deleted Within 15 days
Saved Trips & Itineraries Permanently Deleted Within 15 days
Search History Permanently Deleted Within 15 days
Anonymous Analytics Data Retained (anonymized) Up to 12 months

Note: Anonymous analytics data (collected via Firebase Analytics) that cannot be linked back to your identity may be retained for up to 12 months for service improvement purposes. This data contains no personally identifiable information.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted, you will not be able to recover your saved trips, itineraries, or any other account information. You are welcome to create a new account at any time.

Additional Information

  • You do not need to uninstall the app before requesting deletion.
  • If you signed in using Google Sign-In, we will delete all data stored on our servers. To revoke Trip Buddy's access to your Google account, visit your Google Account Permissions.
  • For any questions about data deletion, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
